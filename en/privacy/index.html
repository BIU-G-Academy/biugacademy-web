Privacy Notice

Version of 19 October 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

BIU.G Academy is an operating unit of BIU-G Holdings LLC (the data controller). Address: [CONTROLLER ADDRESS — TO BE CONFIRMED BEFORE PUBLICATION]. Privacy contact: support@biugacademy.org.

2. What we use the data for

  • To assess your application to the first cohort and contact you about it;
  • To answer support and privacy requests;
  • To produce aggregate statistics (for example, applications per province) that identify no one.

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not make solely automated decisions about your application.

3. What we collect

Required: name, at least one contact (email or phone), province, age range, main language, education level and areas of interest. Without them we cannot assess the application. Optional: whether the number has WhatsApp, preferred contact channel, internet access and device. We also record which version of this notice you accepted and when. We do not store your IP address: it is used only temporarily, in hashed form, to limit abuse (deleted within 48 hours).

4. Minors

Applicants aged 16 or 17 may apply only with the authorisation of a parent or legal guardian. We do not accept applications from anyone under 16.

5. Who receives the data and where it is kept

The data is received and stored by Cloudflare, Inc., which provides hosting, database and bot-protection (Turnstile) services on our behalf. Servers may be outside Angola (for example in the United States or the European Union). Names and contact details are encrypted before storage. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which does not receive form data. Only authorised BIU.G Academy staff have access.

6. How long we keep it

  • Applications: up to 12 months after submission, unless you are admitted (then the programme terms apply);
  • Support messages: 6 months;
  • Record of privacy requests (no plaintext contact data): 24 months, to show they were fulfilled.

Data is deleted automatically at the end of these periods. Technical backups expire within 30 days after that.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, update or delete your data, object to its processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Use the form below or write to support@biugacademy.org. We answer within 30 days (Angolan law sets 60 business days). You may also complain to Angola's Data Protection Agency (APD), apd.ao.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), encryption of contact data, access control and logging of administrative actions. Even so, as on any open network, data travelling over the internet may be exposed to access by unauthorised third parties.

Ask to delete or access your data

Give the email or phone you used in your application. We will confirm your identity through that contact before acting.
