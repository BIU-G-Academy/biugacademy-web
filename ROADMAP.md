# Roadmap

Phased plan for BIU.G Academy infrastructure. Status labels:

| Label         | Meaning                     |
| ------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Completed** | Delivered and maintained    |
| **Active**    | Current focus               |
| **Planned**   | Not started or not deployed |

Nothing in this document implies government approval, accreditation, completed cohorts, or live LMS/analytics/AI unless marked **Completed** or **Operational** in [README.md](README.md).

**Last aligned:** May 2026
